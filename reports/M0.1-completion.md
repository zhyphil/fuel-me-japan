## Delivered

- Explicit location action or manual selection among 47 prefectures; recorded city/address filtering; regional loading, straight-line Haversine ordering within 50 km, list and station details. Location denial, timeout, unavailable/out-of-Japan position, empty coverage and failed loads have recoverable states.

- Five complete locales: en, zh-Hant, ko, zh-Hans, th. Japanese fuel labels remain visible. Unverified vehicle fuel and unknown station/payment/service facts stay unknown; no M0.2 vehicle selector was introduced.
- Offline OSM/Geofabrik importer with typed IDs, area geometry, prefectural membership, validation/count gates, immutable regional JSON, source registry, ODbL attribution and full derivative-data downloads. Separate official reference prices show cash/tax/unit basis, survey/publication dates, source and processing notice.
- Refresh commands and a read-only GitHub candidate workflow: weekly OSM and several checks around official price publication. Successful candidates require checks and review before release; the workflow cannot push or deploy.

All values/dates were independently compared to the original official workbook; all prefecture counts were independently compared to original geometry. These are not station prices or live prices.
