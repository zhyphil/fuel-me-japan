# Help us test Fuel Me Japan

This is a draft invitation and task sheet. It has not been sent or reviewed by a native English speaker.

## Invitation draft

Are you planning a self-drive trip to Hokkaido, Okinawa or Kyushu, or travelling there now? We are looking for 10 international travellers to try Fuel Me Japan on their phones for about 15 minutes.

The website helps you find recorded fuel-station locations, choose a fuel display preference, read refuelling guidance and plan refuelling before returning a rental car. It does not verify your vehicle's fuel, live station prices, current opening status or vehicle entrances.

You do not need to register, pay, share your exact location or provide a rental contract. We would like to observe whether you can complete four tasks and, with your permission, note anonymous usability issues. You can stop at any time. Please test while safely parked, away from an active refuelling operation.

## Participant task sheet

Open [Fuel Me Japan](https://fuel-me-japan.com/en/) and choose the language you normally use. Please try the following without instructions about which buttons to press. We are testing the website, not you.

1. Find a fuel station in your planned travel region. Open its details and explain how you would start navigation. You do not need to open an external map. What can and cannot be confirmed from the recorded information?
2. Imagine that your rental paperwork explicitly says **diesel / 軽油**. Set your default fuel display, reload the page, and then display two fuel types. What does this setting tell you about the actual vehicle?
3. Find the refuelling guide. Explain the difference between **レギュラー**, **ハイオク** and **軽油**, and what you would check before refuelling. Do not operate a real pump for this test.
4. Find the assigned rental return branch: **Toyota New Chitose Airport Poplar** for Hokkaido, **Toyota Naha Airport Seaside** for Okinawa, or **Toyota Fukuoka Airport INTERNATIONAL** for Kyushu. Look at one nearby fuel candidate and explain the sequence from refuelling to returning the vehicle. What does a map point mean? Does straight-line distance tell you driving time?

If you get stuck, say what you expected. Asking for help is useful feedback. After the tasks, tell us which information was missing and whether you would use the site on your trip. Please avoid names, booking numbers, number plates or exact personal location in your feedback.

The moderator will request permission before keeping anonymous notes. Completed records stay outside the public repository and are deleted within 30 days after this round's summary and retest. No recording is necessary. Optional device location remains under your control; map tiles and the site's disclosed Google components may make external network requests, as described on [About](https://fuel-me-japan.com/en/about/).
