# Fuel Japan — M0 License & Compliance
Engineering checklist, not legal advice.

## OpenStreetMap
ODbL. Provide visible `© OpenStreetMap contributors` attribution and license/copyright link.
Track OSM in the source registry.
Before distributing an enhanced/derived POI database, review and satisfy applicable ODbL share-alike obligations. Open does not mean obligation-free.

## Geofabrik
Extract distributor; underlying OSM obligations remain.

## gogo.gs
M0 = outbound links only. No price scraping/caching/republishing. Keep a record of the public linking policy.

## Japanese government price data
Before production ingestion, record exact dataset terms/attribution in registry. Always display source and survey/reference date and say it is a prefectural reference, not station-live price.

## Rental/JAF/brand sites
Public web pages are not automatically reusable databases. Use authoritative pages as references; no bulk proprietary POI copying without compatible rights.

## Privacy
Explicit geolocation action; no default precise-location storage; no precise location in analytics. Reassess privacy/consent before adding advertising trackers.

## Source registry gate
No source reaches production without owner, URL, purpose, terms/license reference, allowed-use assessment, attribution, refresh method and review date.
